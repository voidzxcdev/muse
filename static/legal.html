Code vs content

The MIT license covers Muse source code only. Songs, art, and names you hear or see stay with their owners. Muse does not sell a music catalog.

Operators

If you run your own Muse server, you are the operator. You need a lawful basis for any catalog you plug in. Prefer an official player embed or a catalog you are licensed to stream.
