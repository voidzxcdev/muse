Privacy

Muse is a music player project. You may use it without an account. Muse does not sell recordings and does not display advertising.

Accounts do not collect an email address. Password reset by email is therefore not available. If you lose your password, create a new account.

Account data

If you register, Muse stores:

  • Username, a hashed password, display name, handle, and avatar
  • Likes, playlists, covers, and playback events (used for For You)
  • Friends, chat, and files you send
  • A push endpoint, if you enable notifications
  • A session cookie to keep you signed in

Without an account, Muse still records IP addresses in ordinary server and Cloudflare logs, as most websites do. Those logs rotate. They are not used to build a marketing profile.

Data not collected

  • Email, phone number, or payment details. There is nothing to bill.
  • The recordings themselves. Thumbnails and streams remain with their rights holders.

Legal basis

Account features (library, friends, chat, session cookie, For You) are processed to perform the contract you request when you register (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

Server logs and abuse prevention are processed on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)). You may object by writing to hello@getmuse.eu, or by not using Muse.

The following require consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): desktop YouTube iframe cookies, and push notifications. You can withdraw consent in the same place you granted it.

Cover art is an HTTP image fetch from Google / YouTube CDNs (i.ytimg.com and related hosts). The request includes your IP. Google processes that as its own CDN logs; Muse is not Google’s processor for it. Muse’s legal bases for causing the request are GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) when you use the player you asked for, and Art. 6(1)(f) when you browse logged out (interest: showing search and library covers). Under EU cookie / ePrivacy rules this is a content fetch, not storing or reading a YouTube cookie on your device. The optional banner is only for the desktop iframe, which can set cookies Muse does not control. Reject does not stop thumbnails. Object to hello@ or do not use the site.

Lyrics are looked up from the Muse origin using the track title and artist. Muse may call LRCLIB, lyrics.ovh, textyl, and NetEase Cloud Music. Your account and IP are not sent. That is a content fetch of public lyric text, not a cookie, and not an account transfer.

Your rights

In Settings you can update your profile, download a JSON export, and delete your account. Deletion removes the library, chat files held on the server, and the username. For other requests, write to hello@getmuse.eu. Further detail is under GDPR.

You must be 16 or older to create an account.

Cookies

The session cookie is strictly necessary if you are signed in. Player preferences such as volume are stored on your device. Under EU cookie rules, those do not require a banner consent.

The optional cookie is the desktop YouTube player iframe, and only after Accept. Muse then loads their iframe API, which may set cookies Muse does not control. Until then, and on phone, playback stays on Muse’s own audio. Both options work. There are no ads.

Thumbnails still load from Google/YouTube CDNs without that Accept. That is an image GET, not the iframe, and it does not store a YouTube cookie on your device. The request includes your IP, which Google may log. GDPR bases are in Privacy. Reject does not hide artwork.

You can change this later with Reject, the desktop Settings toggle, or by clearing site data.

Uptime

Availability is measured. It is not advertised as 99.99%. Muse is free; missed time is not compensated. Outages at YouTube, Cloudflare, or the catalog are excluded.

The target is 99.0% of minutes in a UTC calendar month on GET /api/health. A tick is recorded each minute. A missing tick counts as downtime for that minute.
